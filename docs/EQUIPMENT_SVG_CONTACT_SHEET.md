# Equipment SVG contact sheet

This labeled contact sheet is the human-review surface for all 139 retained
equipment SVGs after the August 24, 2026 consistency sweep.

The sheet is ready for human review. Requested physical-form, canonical-reuse,
projection, and layer-order corrections are implemented. It was regenerated
after final independent repaired-asset review, so it is a snapshot of the
current 139-asset source tree.

[figures/final_equipment_contact_sheet.svg](figures/final_equipment_contact_sheet.svg)
is the canonical full-resolution figure. Open that link directly to zoom or
download it without rasterizing the labels. The figure is self-contained and
has no external image dependencies.

The sheet groups assets by authored behavior and labels every source folder and
filename. Seven result composites are visibly marked `DEFERRED / BYTE-PRESERVED`;
their migration to application-owned result interfaces remains in
[TODO.md](TODO.md) and [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).
